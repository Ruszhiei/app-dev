# My Favorite Series Movie
**Title:** Alice in Borderland
**Type:** Series (Live Action)
**Language:** Japanese
**Author:** Haso Aso (Also a Manga Artist)
---
## Why I like it
1. The **survival games** are intense and unpredictable.
2. The storytelling is **smart and emotional**, full of twist and deep meaning.
3. The **visual effects and pacing** build up great suspense.
   >"Survival isn't just about winning - It's about choosing what kind of person you'll be"
 [title](https://medium.com/@LostInTranslation_/what-alice-in-borderland-taught-me-about-the-meaning-of-life-8d45262b2af3)

---
## Summary
*Alice in Borderland* The show tells the story about multiple people who have suddenly been transported into an alternative reality, 
where they are forced to play deadly games in order to survive. Every game provides them with a visa, which prolongs their stay, while 
they are searching for a way to return to the world they are used to.
